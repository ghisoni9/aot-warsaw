Get involved

Astronomy on Tap Warsaw only happens because people volunteer their evenings. There are three ways in.

Give a talk

If you do research in astronomy, astrophysics, cosmology or anything close, we would love to have you. Talks are about 10 minutes, in English, for an audience with no background. No equations, lots of pictures, and a real question at the heart of it.

We run a rehearsal with every speaker beforehand, so a first-time speaker is very welcome.

What makes a good Astronomy on Tap talk

One question

Build the talk around a single question somebody in a pub might actually ask: how do we find black holes? Can AI do astronomy?

Your own work

The audience wants to meet a scientist, not read a textbook. What you do on a normal working day is more interesting than you think.

Room for interruptions

People ask questions in the middle. That is the point of the format, so leave space for it.
