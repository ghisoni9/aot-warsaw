What happens on the night

Two talks

Two researchers, about 20 minutes each, on what they work on: black holes, dust, supernovae, dark energy, machine learning in astronomy.

A quiz

A team quiz between the talks, played on your phone, with real prizes. Questions range from serious astronomy to how many beers fit in a telescope.

Questions, all night

The speakers stay for a drink afterwards. If you have ever wanted to corner an astronomer and ask about aliens, this is the place.
